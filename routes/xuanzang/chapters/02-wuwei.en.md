Liangzhou corresponds broadly to modern Wuwei. Zoom out to see the corridor between the Qilian Mountains and the dry regions to their north. This landscape channels long-distance movement.

Why follow towns and oases? Water, food, contacts and information make a settlement much more than a point on a map.
