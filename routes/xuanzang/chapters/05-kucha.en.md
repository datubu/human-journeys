Kucha, broadly associated with modern Kuqa, is an important place for understanding Buddhist culture in the Western Regions. Travel between oases also meant encounters with different languages, arts and intellectual traditions.

The map connects modern reference areas. It does not reconstruct streets or precise ancient paths. Exchange took place in teaching, discussion and everyday contact that a line cannot show.
