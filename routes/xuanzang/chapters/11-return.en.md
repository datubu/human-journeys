Xuanzang returned to Chang’an in 645 and devoted years to translating Buddhist texts. A map can end at the capital; the movement of knowledge continued from there.

Looking back, the point is not to compress a life into a line, but to use documented places, people and records to understand how travel brings distant worlds into conversation.
