The return passed through Khotan, broadly associated with modern Hotan. A traveller who left with questions returned with learning and texts: the journey carried a different weight.

The return was not a replay of the outward path. This map selects Khotan as one return node and omits many intermediate places and travels within India. It is not a complete daily itinerary.
