Near the western end of the Hexi Corridor, dry terrain makes provisions central to any reading of a journey. Modern Guazhou is a regional reference, not a claim about an exact historical stop or water source.

Observe the contrast between cultivated land and desert. Today’s irrigation is not evidence of Tang-period conditions, but the landscape helps explain why straight-line distance alone tells so little.
