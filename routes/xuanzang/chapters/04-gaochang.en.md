Gaochang is a significant stop in accounts of the westward journey. Travel depended not only on determination, but also on hospitality, assistance and relationships. Oasis cities brought people from different backgrounds together.

The question shifts from how far one person travelled to who made that travel possible. The details of meetings, support and stays belong to close reading of records and scholarship.
