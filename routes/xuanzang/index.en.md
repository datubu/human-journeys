## A journey for knowledge

From an imperial city to oases, Central Asian valleys and a South Asian centre of learning. Follow 11 selected chapters through Xuanzang’s outward journey, study and return. Narrative, people, places and references share one content record.

## Reading this route

This account adopts the familiar 629–645 chronology. Sources differ on the departure year; the archive also includes scholarship using 627 for comparison.

The map is a regional schematic of selected places. It omits many intermediate stops and journeys within India. Coordinates refer to modern places; connecting lines are not surveyed ancient paths, national borders or present-day roads.

## Visiting today

Begin with museums, heritage sites and official information. The case links to official references for Nalanda and the memorial hall. It does not offer a continuous cross-border walking itinerary.
