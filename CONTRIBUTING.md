# Contributing

Propose corrections through an issue or pull request. Keep a stable ID, add source references, supply both Chinese and English, and explain spatial or chronological uncertainty. Do not describe a historical schematic as a verified modern trail.

Chapter order must be consecutive; coordinates are [longitude, latitude]. Source IDs must resolve to records in `sources/`. Do not embed scripts, raw HTML, credentials, or unlicensed third-party material in content. Route records need `status: published` to appear publicly.
